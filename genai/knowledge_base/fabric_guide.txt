COTTON — The Fabric of Kerala Life
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
"Cotton is what Kerala's mornings feel like — fresh, honest, dependable."

If you live in Kerala, cotton is not just a fabric. It is survival.
Our humidity demands it. Our lifestyle loves it.

Feel: Soft, breathable, naturally cooling
Best occasions: Daily wear, office, summer festivals, morning markets
Best season: Summer and spring — the fabric was made for our climate
Care: Machine wash on gentle cycle, tumble dry low — cotton forgives
Ironing: Medium heat — it loves a good press
Shrinkage note: Cotton may shrink 5 to 10 percent on first wash.
  Always wash before getting your fabric stitched at our boutique.
Price impact: Most affordable — beauty at everyday prices
Anjali recommendation: Best for our daily kurtas, palazzo bottoms,
  casual tops, and our iconic Kerala Kasavu cotton sarees for Onam

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SILK — The Fabric of Celebrations
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
"Silk remembers every occasion it was worn to. So does the woman wearing it."

There are moments in life that call for silk. A daughter's wedding.
A mother's felicitation. Diwali at the ancestral home. For those
moments, only silk will do — and we know exactly which silk.

Feel: Smooth, lustrous, weighted with elegance
Best occasions: Weddings, festivals, formal events, sarees, bridal blouses
Best season: October to February — festival and wedding season
Care: Dry clean only, or cold hand wash with extreme gentleness
Ironing: Low heat with a cloth buffer between iron and fabric.
  Never iron silk directly — it will mark and never recover.
Price impact: Premium — but a silk saree from us will outlast trends
Natural silk test: Real silk burns slowly with a smell like burnt hair
  and leaves a crushable ash. Synthetic silk burns fast and melts.
Anjali recommendation: Our festive sarees, bridal blouses, special
  occasion kurtas — this is where silk belongs

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
GEORGETTE — The Fabric of Movement
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
"Georgette does not just move. It dances."

Watch a georgette saree walk into a room. It announces itself.
It flows, it catches light, it commands attention without trying.

Feel: Lightweight, flowing, slightly crinkled texture
Best occasions: Evening events, dupattas, sarees, layered dresses
Best season: All seasons but especially beautiful at indoor events
Care: Dry clean preferred — or very gentle hand wash in cold water
Ironing: Low heat only — georgette is delicate with heat
Price impact: Mid-range — the perfect balance of elegance and value
Anjali recommendation: Our evening sets, function sarees, and any
  outfit where we want fabric to work with the woman, not against her

CHIFFON — The Fabric of Dreams
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
"Chiffon is what whispers look like."

The most ethereal of our fabrics. Translucent, weightless, poetic.
A chiffon dupatta over a rich silk blouse is pure magic.

Feel: Ultra-lightweight, sheer, incredibly floaty
Best for: Dupattas, layered sarees, special occasion overlays
Important note: Chiffon is transparent — it always needs a lining
  for blouses and dresses. We handle this in our stitching.
Anjali recommendation: Best as dupatta fabric or saree drape material

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
RAYON (Viscose) — The Everyday Luxury
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
"Rayon looks expensive. It is not. That is the secret."

Feel: Soft, smooth, almost silky — without the silk price tag
Best for: Casual kurtas, comfortable palazzo sets, summer dresses
Best season: Summer — it breathes almost as well as cotton
Care: Machine wash cold, lay flat to dry to keep the shape
Price impact: Very affordable, excellent value for money
Anjali recommendation: Best for our everyday sets and casual kurtas
  that customers wear most often and love most faithfully

CREPE — The Professional's Choice
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
"Crepe says: I am here. I am professional. I am not going to wrinkle."

For the working woman who moves between client meetings and evening
events without going home to change — crepe is the answer.

Feel: Slightly textured, drapes beautifully, resists wrinkles
Best for: Office wear, semi-formal events, professional kurtas
Best season: All seasons — ideal for air-conditioned environments
Care: Dry clean or cold hand wash
Price impact: Mid-range — excellent for building a work wardrobe
Anjali recommendation: Our work-ready kurtas and tops that travel

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
BROCADE — The Fabric of Royalty
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
"Brocade does not whisper. It announces."

Heavy, richly woven with metallic threads, brocade carries the
weight of tradition and the brilliance of celebration.

Best for: Bridal blouses, lehenga borders, festive kurtas
Best season: October to February — weddings and winter festivals
Care: Dry clean only — brocade does not forgive water
Price impact: Premium — this is investment dressing
Anjali recommendation: Our bridal blouse collection — the brocade
  blouse we stitch for a bride is often the garment her daughter
  will want to wear someday too

LINEN — Kerala's Summer Answer
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
"Linen wrinkles. Real life wrinkles too. Both are beautiful."

Feel: Textured, airy, natural — the most honest fabric there is
Best for: Summer kurtas, casual tops, palazzo
Best season: Summer — perfect for Kerala's heat and humidity
Care: Machine wash cold, air dry — linen is practical
Ironing: High heat with steam — linen responds beautifully to steam
Anjali recommendation: Our summer daily wear collection — linen
  makes every morning feel like a fresh start
