ONAM — Kerala's Most Sacred Festival
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
"Onam without a Kasavu saree is like a pookalam without marigolds."

For Anjali Ladies Boutique in Muggam, Onam is the most important
festival of the year. This is when Kerala's soul speaks, and the
Kasavu saree is its language.

Our Onam Kasavu sarees: Cream Kerala cotton with pure gold zari borders.
This is Kerala's identity in fabric form. Families return to us every
single year for their Onam sarees, and that loyalty is our deepest honor.

What to wear for Onam:
Women: Kasavu saree — the cream and gold combination is sacred to Onam.
  Or a white and gold set for those who prefer non-saree options.
Young girls: White kurta with gold dupatta, or white set with gold border
Men and boys: White mundu with gold border

Colors sacred to Onam: White and gold — this is Kerala tradition.
  Other festival colors like yellow, cream, and off-white are also lovely.

Our Onam preparation: Stock up from late July. August is when Onam
  demand peaks for us. Order your Kasavu saree early — we sell out.
Lead time for Onam custom orders: 3 weeks minimum. Do not wait.

DIWALI — The Festival of Light and Color
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
"On Diwali, the world lights up. Dress like you are one of the lights."

Diwali is our biggest mainland festival season. Families come to us
in September and October wanting to look radiant by November.

The Diwali palette: Gold, orange, deep red, magenta pink, emerald green,
  royal purple, saffron yellow — every color of a lit diya
Best categories for Diwali: Sarees for the formal puja, sets for the
  family gathering, ethnic dresses for the Diwali party evening
Best fabrics: Silk and georgette — they catch the light from the diyas
  and make you glow from within
Embroidery for Diwali: Zari catches candlelight beautifully, sequins
  and mirror work sparkle like the festival itself
Colors to avoid at Diwali: White and black are traditionally avoided
  during the auspicious celebrations
Lead time for custom Diwali orders: 2 weeks minimum
Stock up timing: September and October — demand peaks 2 weeks before Diwali

NAVRATRI — Nine Nights of Dance and Color
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
"Nine nights. Nine colors. Infinite ways to shine."

The Navratri color calendar:
Night 1 Yellow. Night 2 Green. Night 3 Grey. Night 4 Orange.
Night 5 White. Night 6 Red. Night 7 Royal Blue. Night 8 Pink.
Night 9 Purple. Each night its own story.

Best categories for Navratri: Sets for garba dancing — they move with
  you and keep their shape through hours of dance. Ethnic dresses.
  Chaniya choli style outfits.
Fabric for Navratri: Lightweight cotton or georgette — you will be
  dancing for hours. Comfort is not optional, it is essential.
Special tip from us: Your footwear matters as much as your outfit for
  Navratri. Make sure you can dance in what you choose.
Stock up timing: August and September

WEDDINGS — The Most Important Outfits
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
"At a wedding, everyone is watching. We make sure you are worth watching."

For the bride: This is the most important outfit of your life.
  At Anjali Boutique, we dedicate ourselves entirely to the bride's vision.
  Our bridal silk sarees, embroidered blouses, and custom lehenga sets are
  crafted with the care your day deserves.
  Colors: Red and gold are classic, but today's brides choose everything
  from soft pink to deep maroon to royal teal. Your wedding, your rules.
  Start your bridal consultation 6 to 8 weeks before the wedding.

For close family: Saree or a coordinated set in rich fabrics.
  Silk sarees for mothers. Georgette sets for sisters and cousins.
  The family looks best when their outfits complement but do not match
  the bride.

For friends of the bride or groom: Kurta set, ethnic dress, or western
  dress for younger guests. Saree for senior guests.

Guest color etiquette:
  Avoid white at Hindu weddings — it is the color of mourning.
  Avoid red — that is the bride's color in many traditions.
  Safe and beautiful choices: Gold, navy, emerald, purple, champagne,
  teal, deep pink — rich, celebratory, never upstaging.

CHRISTMAS AND NEW YEAR — The December Magic
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
"December is the only month that deserves two wardrobes."

In Kerala's Christian communities, Christmas is celebrated with the
same devotion and joy as Onam. And New Year's Eve is everyone's party.

Christmas style: Rich reds and forest greens, warm gold details.
  Velvet blouses and silk sarees for church. Comfortable sets for family
  gatherings. The modest and the beautiful can absolutely coexist.
New Year style: This is the night for shimmer. Silver, gold, midnight
  blue, deep black with sparkle. Our sequin sets and western dresses
  fly off the racks in the last week of December.
Our December rush: We begin stocking western dresses and party sets
  from November. If you want something specific for New Year, come
  to us by the first week of December.

OFFICE WEAR — The Power of Quiet Elegance
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
"You can be professional and beautiful. We never asked you to choose."

The working women of Kerala are our daily heroes. They want to look
polished without trying too hard, professional without being dull.

Best choices: Cotton or crepe kurta with trouser, or tops with palazzo.
  The A-line kurta over straight pants is eternally office-appropriate.
Colors for office: Navy, charcoal grey, white, beige, soft pastels.
  Avoid overly bright festival colors and heavy embroidery for daily work.
Fabrics that work: Cotton breathes, crepe travels, linen makes a
  statement. All three handle the Kerala heat with grace.
