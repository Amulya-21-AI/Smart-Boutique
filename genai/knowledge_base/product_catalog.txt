"A well-stitched kurta is not just clothing — it is an attitude."

At Anjali Boutique, the kurta is our most beloved creation. From the
woman who needs a crisp cotton kurta for Monday morning office to the
mother who wants an embroidered silk kurta for her daughter's engagement
— we have a kurta for every chapter of her life.

Our master embroiderers hand-stitch every Chikankari and Phulkari
detail. No machine can replicate the love poured into each stitch.

Occasions: Daily wear, office, festivals, family functions, casual outings
Price: Rs 300 to Rs 900 — beauty does not have to break the bank
Sizes: XS through 3XL — because every body is a beautiful body
Fabrics we love for kurtas: Cotton breathes like Kerala air, silk is for
  the moments that matter, georgette is for when you want to float
Colors that define our kurta collection: Deep navy, turmeric yellow,
  Kerala green, temple red, ivory white — colors borrowed from our land
Embroidery signatures: Chikankari is the whisper of white on white,
  Phulkari brings Punjab's fields blooming on fabric, mirror work
  catches every eye in the room, thread embroidery is our grandmother's
  art revived with love
Season suitability: All year — cotton kurtas in summer heat, silk kurtas
  when the festival season calls in October and November
Custom stitching available: Yes — bring your fabric or choose ours

THE SAREE — Our Kerala Heritage

"A saree is not worn. It is draped — like a poem around the body."

In Kerala, a saree is not just clothing — it is identity, culture,
and celebration woven into six magnificent yards. At Anjali Boutique,
we treat every saree with the reverence it deserves.

Our Onam Kasavu sarees are legendary in Muggam. Cream cotton with gold
borders — the embodiment of Kerala's soul. Every year, families return
to us for their Onam sarees, and that trust is our greatest honor.

We source directly from weavers. When you buy a saree from Anjali
Boutique, you are also supporting the hands that wove it with devotion.

Occasions: Weddings the most important day in someone's life, Onam the
  soul of Kerala, Diwali, Navratri, office formal, religious ceremonies
Price: Rs 600 to Rs 2000 — a saree is an investment not an expense
Size: Free size — the magic of six yards fits everyone beautifully
Our finest fabrics: Pure silk for weddings and legacy moments, Kerala
  cotton for Onam's heartbeat, georgette for festivals and functions,
  Chanderi for the refined woman, Banarasi for when you want the whole
  room to pause and look
Embroidery we offer: Zari with gold woven into every dream, Kalamkari
  with stories painted on fabric, Bandhani with Gujarat's tie-dye soul,
  block print for handmade authenticity
Peak demand: October through December — Navratri, Diwali, wedding season
