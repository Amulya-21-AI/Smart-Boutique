HOW TO TAKE YOUR MEASUREMENTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
"Two minutes with a measuring tape saves hours of disappointment."

Bust: Wrap the tape around the fullest part of your chest,
  keeping it parallel to the floor. Breathe normally.
Waist: Measure around the narrowest part of your torso,
  usually one inch above the navel. Do not hold your breath.
Hip: Measure around the fullest part of your hips and seat,
  about 8 inches below your natural waist.

Our golden rule: Always take measurements over your inner clothing,
not over a thick outfit. And if in doubt, call us — our team in
Muggam is always happy to guide you.

ANJALI FIT TIPS — EARNED FROM EXPERIENCE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
"We have fitted thousands of women across Kerala. Here is what we know."

When you are between two sizes, always go one size up for comfort.
The best outfit is the one you can breathe, sit, and dance in.

For kurtas: Size by your bust measurement first.
For sarees: They are free size — the six yards work for every woman.
  The blouse is where the fit matters most. Always custom-stitch your blouse.
For palazzo and salwar: Size by your hip measurement primarily.
For western dresses: Check bust first, but also verify the hip
  especially for bodycon and A-line styles.
For blouses: We always recommend custom stitching at our boutique.
  A blouse stitched to your exact measurements transforms how a saree
  drapes on you. It is the single most impactful fit investment you can make.

FABRIC AND STRETCH — WHAT TO EXPECT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Cotton has no stretch so size up if you are between sizes.
Georgette flows but does not stretch so stick to your exact size.
Rayon drapes beautifully and is true to size.
Lycra and spandex blend has excellent stretch so you can size down.
Crepe has no stretch and is true to standard size.
